Where is the cat?

Open Keyloaf and click its keyboard-and-cat icon in the menu bar. In Appearance, enable Show cat. Use Reset position if the cat is out of view.

Why isn’t it typing along?

Open Animations and choose Tap a key. To enable typing feedback in other apps, turn on React to typing in other apps in Input & Privacy, then allow Keyloaf in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Input Monitoring. Reopen Keyloaf if macOS asks. Keyboard feedback pauses while macOS Secure Input is active.

How do I hide key names while recording?

Turn off Show floating keycaps in Input & Privacy. To also hide identifying key highlights and pause typing estimates, enable Hide key identities. Turn off React to typing in other apps to stop global keyboard feedback.

How do I get my Plus coats back?

Open Plus in Settings and click Restore purchases. Use the Apple Account that originally bought Keyloaf Plus. The black cat remains available for free. Keyloaf Plus unlocks six extra coats in a single purchase.

How do I clear typing estimates?

Open Appearance and select Reset counts… to clear both daily and lifetime estimates.
