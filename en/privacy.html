Keyloaf Privacy Policy

This policy applies to Keyloaf for macOS.

Keyboard and pointer input

Keyloaf uses pointer movement, clicks, scrolling, and keystrokes on your Mac to animate the cat. When Show floating keycaps is enabled, recognized physical keys may appear briefly as keycaps. Keyloaf does not read committed input method text or save key sequences, typed content, or pointer trails.

React to typing in other apps is optional. To react while you type in other apps, Keyloaf needs you to grant macOS Input Monitoring permission. You can turn off global feedback in Input & Privacy or revoke permission in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Input Monitoring. You can also turn off keycap labels separately. Hide key identities suppresses key identification, corresponding highlights, and estimated typing counts. Keyloaf pauses keyboard feedback and counting while macOS Secure Input is active. Keyloaf also recognizes the password field in its own settings for demonstration.

The preview distributed separately from the Mac App Store can optionally use macOS Accessibility permission to check whether the focused field in another app is marked as a password field. It checks the field type and does not read its value. You can revoke this permission in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility. The Mac App Store edition uses Secure Input and its own password demonstration without requesting Accessibility access.

Local settings and typing estimates

Keyloaf stores preferences such as coat, keyboard style, position, and size on your Mac. It also stores a date and daily and lifetime typing estimates. These figures are based on printable key presses. The daily estimate resets on a new day. Lifetime totals and settings stay on your Mac until you remove them. Use Reset counts… in Appearance to remove your typing estimates. To remove other settings, quit and uninstall the app, then remove the local data kept by macOS for Keyloaf.

Network access and sharing

Desktop companion features run on your Mac. Keyloaf does not require an account and does not use advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs. It does not send keystrokes, pointer activity, typing estimates, or app settings to the developer or to third parties.

Keyloaf Plus purchases

The free black cat works without a purchase. The app uses Apple’s StoreKit to check product and entitlement information. If you choose to buy or restore Keyloaf Plus, Apple processes the transaction through the App Store. Keyloaf uses StoreKit purchase status on your Mac to unlock Plus features. The developer does not receive your payment card details or Apple Account password, and Keyloaf does not send purchase information to a developer server. Apple's handling of purchase and payment information is described in Apple's privacy policy.
